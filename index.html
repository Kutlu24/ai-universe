About AI Universe

AI Universe is a small, free directory of real AI tools in 8 categories. Every entry links to the tool's own site; nothing is invented or sponsored.

How are the tools chosen?

Each entry is a real, currently operating product, hand-checked against its official site.

What do the price tiers mean?

"Free tier" means the tool can be used without paying; "paid" means a subscription or payment is required.

All tools

  • ChatGPT (chat, free tier): OpenAI's general-purpose chat assistant, with GPT models, browsing, and image generation built in.
  • Claude (chat, free tier): Anthropic's assistant, built around Claude models - strong at writing, reasoning, and coding.
  • Gemini (chat, free tier): Google's assistant, tightly integrated with Search, Workspace, and Android.
  • Perplexity (research, free tier): An answer engine that searches the live web and cites its sources inline.
  • Le Chat (chat, free tier): Mistral AI's chat assistant, built on their open-weight model family.
  • Midjourney (image, paid): High-fidelity, stylized image generation, historically Discord-first, now with its own web app.
  • Stable Diffusion (image, free tier): Stability AI's open-weight image model family - runs locally or via many hosted front-ends.
  • Adobe Firefly (image, free tier): Adobe's generative image/design suite, integrated into Photoshop and Illustrator.
  • Ideogram (image, free tier): Image generation particularly strong at rendering legible text inside images.
  • Leonardo AI (image, free tier): Image generation aimed at game assets and production-ready art pipelines.
  • Runway (video, paid): Text-to-video and video-editing AI tools, including the Gen video model family.
  • Pika (video, free tier): Text- and image-to-video generation focused on short, stylized clips.
  • Synthesia (video, paid): AI avatar video generation for training and explainer videos, from a text script.
  • HeyGen (video, free tier): AI avatar and voice-cloned video generation, popular for multilingual marketing video.
  • Luma Dream Machine (video, free tier): Fast text/image-to-video generation from Luma AI.
  • ElevenLabs (audio, free tier): Realistic text-to-speech and voice cloning, widely used for narration and dubbing.
  • Suno (audio, free tier): Generates full songs - vocals and instrumentation - from a text prompt.
  • Udio (audio, free tier): Another text-to-music generator, competing directly with Suno on song quality.
  • Descript (audio, paid): Edits audio/video by editing a text transcript - includes AI voice cloning and filler-word removal.
  • GitHub Copilot (code, paid): In-editor AI pair programmer from GitHub/Microsoft, built into most major IDEs.
  • Cursor (code, free tier): An AI-native code editor (a VS Code fork) built around multi-file, agentic edits.
  • Claude Code (code, paid): Anthropic's terminal- and IDE-based coding agent, built on Claude models.
  • Replit (code, free tier): Browser-based coding environment with an AI agent that can scaffold and run whole apps.
  • v0 (code, free tier): Vercel's tool for generating React/Next.js UI components from a text or image prompt.
  • Grammarly (writing, free tier): Grammar, tone, and clarity assistant that works inline across most apps and browsers.
  • Jasper (writing, paid): AI writing assistant aimed at marketing teams - brand voice, campaigns, long-form content.
  • Notion AI (writing, paid): Writing, summarizing, and Q&A built directly into Notion pages and databases.
  • NotebookLM (research, free tier): Google's tool for asking questions across your own uploaded documents, grounded in citations.
  • Elicit (research, free tier): Searches and summarizes academic papers to help answer a specific research question.
  • Consensus (research, free tier): A search engine over peer-reviewed research, surfacing consensus across papers on a claim.
  • Make (agents, free tier): Visual workflow automation platform (formerly Integromat), with AI-app integrations.
